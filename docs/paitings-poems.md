
# The Second Moon
Second Moon is watching
so is the sleeping forest
In the depths of reflection
Of a lake long forgotten

# The Last Ember 
The Last Ember fades
in times where hope is scarce
but there is yet hope
as the new day begins

# Coming of the Second Sun

Upon the Peak where the Star fell
An observatory stands to watch the universe
With the First Stone it manipulates the world
To bring the birth of the Second Sun

# The Moon

Always watching, yet it never blinks.
A guide to the stars, and a whisper of the forest.
The one who has seen what was, what is, and what may yet be.

# Wish you were here

As the Sun sets upon the mountain view,
In its light I see what I love most,
The warmth, comfort and beauty
Of simple kindness and compassion.

# Wake of the Last God

In times where all hope has withered, and the air has ceased to blow.
The moon points the way,
To the Wake of the Last God

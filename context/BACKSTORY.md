## Who
Interdisciplinary artist: painter, poet, multi-instrumentalist musician (piano, guitar). Also a professional software engineer  -  Master's degree, Warsaw University of Technology. Based in Poland; intends to move toward Paris, France. Works a full-time office job; paints in free time.

## The work
Sits at the intersection of Dark Romanticism, Surrealism, and dystopian foresight. Academic/classical technique (Old Masters influence, chiaroscuro, oil painting) applied to human hubris, existential decay, and ruined future landscapes. Music is an ontological extension of the paintings  -  a sonic landscape parallel to the visual ruined world (classical counterpoint + modern instrumentation). Feeds independent dark-dystopian narrative/game-design projects (lore + music + concept art as one whole).

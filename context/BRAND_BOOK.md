## Artistic positioning
Dark Romanticism × Surrealism × dystopian foresight. Old Masters rigor (chiaroscuro, oil) applied to human hubris, existential decay, ruined future landscapes. Themes: The Hubris of Man · The Subversion of the Madonna · Eros vs. Thanatos. Always in pursuit of the Sublime.
